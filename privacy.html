Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Unrust is a mobility-first workout logger. This page explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: the email address you sign in with, or the identifier Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Your profile: display name, handle, optional bio and profile photo, and whether your profile is public.
  • Your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, hold times, range-of-motion measurements, titles, notes and any photos you attach.
  • Your mobility sprints and assessments: the pain points you choose and the before/after measurements you record.
  • Social activity: who you follow and who follows you, likes, comments, blocks, hidden posts and reports you file.
  • Settings: units, weekly goal, default post visibility and notification choices.
  • Push notification tokens, if you allow notifications.

Apple Health: if you turn it on in Settings, Unrust saves your finished workouts to Apple Health. The app only writes to Health. It never reads your Health data.

Why we collect it

To run the app: log and show your workouts, track your progress, let you share with people you choose, send the notifications you ask for, and keep the community safe (reports, blocks and moderation).

Who can see what

  • Every workout has its own visibility: Private (only you), Followers (people whose follow you have approved), or Public (anyone, and only if your profile is public).
  • Mobility sprint workouts default to Private because they can reveal a health issue.
  • A private profile shows only your name, handle, photo and a Private badge to people who do not follow you.
  • People you block cannot see your profile, workouts or comments, and you cannot see theirs.
  • Photos are stored privately and are only shown to people who are allowed to see the workout they belong to.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts the database, file storage and sign-in. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's push service, which receives your device push token and the notification text.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile, Settings, Delete account. Deleting removes your profile, workouts, photos, routines, social activity and sign-in. You can also delete individual workouts, photos and comments yourself.

Children

Unrust is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
